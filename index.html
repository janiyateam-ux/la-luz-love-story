A LETTER TO YOU

Dear La Luz...

Umugwaneza,

Happy birthday to the woman who somehow became so much more than I ever expected her to be. ❤️

Sometimes I sit and think about us, and I realize how beautiful it is that our story did not begin with romance. It began with friendship. Back in 2019, I met you simply as someone who would become a part of my life. I had no idea then that years later, you would become the person I would call my Wifey, my La Luz, and one of the most important people in my world.

If someone had told me back then that the girl I was getting to know would one day hold such a special place in my heart, I probably would not have believed them. But life has a funny way of giving us people we never knew we needed.

And somehow, it gave me you.

La Luz, there are so many things I could say about you, but sometimes words feel too small. I could tell you that you are beautiful, but beauty doesn't come close to describing what makes you special to me. I could tell you that I love you, but even those three words don't completely explain everything I feel when I think about you.

You have become part of my memories, my happiness, my thoughts, my plans, and the little moments in between. You are someone whose presence can change the feeling of an ordinary day. Sometimes just knowing that you are there is enough to make everything feel a little lighter.

I don't love you only for the beautiful moments. I love you for the real ones too. The laughter, the conversations, the misunderstandings, the moments when we don't agree, the moments when we miss each other, and the moments when we find our way back to each other.

Because love, to me, isn't about pretending that everything will always be perfect. It is about choosing each other even when life isn't perfect. It is about learning, growing, forgiving, understanding, and continuing to say, "I'm still here."

And through everything, I am grateful that I get to say that about us.

You are not just my girlfriend. You are my Wifey. You are the person I can look at and think, "This is someone I want beside me." Someone I want to create memories with. Someone I want to see smile. Someone whose happiness genuinely matters to me.

I hope you know that you deserve to be loved gently. You deserve to be appreciated, respected, listened to, protected, encouraged, and reminded of your worth. And I hope that in the years ahead, I can continue becoming the kind of man who gives you that kind of love.

Today is your birthday, but honestly, I feel like I am the one who has been given a gift.

Because somewhere along the way, I got you.

I got your smile. I got your laughter. I got your little ways. I got the memories we have already created and all the memories we haven't created yet. I got someone who makes my heart feel things that I cannot always explain.

And I don't take that for granted.

I don't know exactly what the future has written for us. None of us can see that far ahead. But I know what I want whenever I imagine my future: I want more moments with you. More conversations. More laughter. More adventures. More birthdays. More pictures. More late nights. More ordinary days that somehow become special simply because we shared them.

I want to keep learning you. I want to keep discovering the little things that make you happy. I want to celebrate your victories, stand beside you when things are difficult, and remind you of who you are whenever you forget.

And if I ever fail to say it enough, let this letter say it for me:

You matter to me. More than you probably realize.

You have a place in my heart that nobody else could simply walk into. That place belongs to you because of the history we have, the friendship that started everything, the love we found, and every little moment that brought us here.

So on your birthday, I don't just want to wish you happiness. I want to remind you that you are worthy of it. I want you to chase your dreams without being afraid. I want you to believe in yourself even on the days when it is difficult. I want you to know that your existence makes this world more beautiful.

And selfishly, I want to be around to see the woman you continue becoming.

Thank you for being my La Luz. Thank you for being my Wifey. Thank you for being someone who started as a friend in 2019 and somehow became such an important part of my heart.

Whatever happens, I will always be grateful that our paths crossed.

Happy birthday, my love.

May this new chapter of your life bring you peace where you need peace, courage where you need courage, success where you seek it, and happiness that stays long after the birthday candles are gone.

And whenever you look back at this little website, I hope you remember one simple thing:

Someone loved you enough to turn his feelings into a little corner of the internet just so you could have something that was completely yours.

I love you, Umugwaneza.

Happy birthday, La Luz. ❤️

Forever your Wifey's favorite person. ❤️
